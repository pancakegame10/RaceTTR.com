Parents & volunteers

the crew behind the crew

Karting runs on family. Between our Junior, Senior, and Masters drivers, race weekends take more hands than just the ones on the wheel — from timing stand to pit lane to the drive home. Here's where parents and volunteers fit in, and how to jump in at whatever level works for you.

Ways to volunteer

pick a role, big or small

Pit crew support

Help with tire changes, fueling, and getting a kart from the trailer to grid on time. No experience needed — our crew chief will walk you through it.

Timing & scoring

Track lap times and results from the stands so drivers and coaches can review pace between sessions.

Transport & logistics

Help coordinate rides, trailer towing, or hauling gear to and from out-of-town races.

Fundraising & sponsor outreach

Help find local sponsors, run a fundraiser, or manage donations that keep tires, fuel, and entry fees covered.

Photography & content

Capture race weekend photos and short clips for the gallery and team socials — a camera or phone is all you need.

Event day setup

Arrive early to help set up the pit area, canopy, and tools, and help break it all down again after the last race.

Support from the stands

just as valuable, no sign-up required

Show up and cheer

Every driver, races better with familiar faces trackside. Showing up matters more than you'd think.

Follow & share

Like, comment, and share race recaps and photos — it helps the team's reach and makes sponsors take notice.

Welcome new families

If you've been around the paddock a while, help newer TTR families get oriented on race weekend routines.

Spread the word

Know a local business that might want to sponsor, or a driver looking for a team? A quick introduction goes a long way.

Want to get involved?

Let us know what interests you and how much time you have — even an hour on race day helps.
